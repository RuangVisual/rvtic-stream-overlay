RVTik StreamCam

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RVTik StreamCam handles data, device permissions, and user configuration when you use our Android application.

Summary: RVTik StreamCam does not sell user data. The app processes camera input, microphone audio, USB/UVC devices, overlays, LUT files, and streaming configuration only to provide camera preview and live streaming features. Video and audio data are sent only to the RTMP/RTMPS destination configured by the user.

1. About RVTik StreamCam

RVTik StreamCam is an Android application designed for camera monitoring and live streaming. The app may be used with the internal Android camera, USB/UVC cameras, HDMI capture devices, microphones, overlays, LUT color filters, and streaming servers that support RTMP or RTMPS.

2. Data Processed by the App

RVTik StreamCam may process the following types of data to provide its features:

  • Camera input from the internal Android camera.
  • Camera input from USB/UVC cameras or HDMI capture devices.
  • Microphone audio when audio streaming is enabled by the user.
  • RTMP or RTMPS server URL and stream key entered by the user.
  • App settings such as resolution, FPS, bitrate, orientation, LUT, and overlay configuration.
  • Local files selected by the user, such as PNG overlays, GIF overlays, or LUT files.

3. How the Data Is Used

The data is used only to provide app functionality, including:

  • Displaying camera preview inside the app.
  • Accessing the selected USB/UVC camera or capture device.
  • Capturing microphone audio when the user enables audio streaming.
  • Processing video, LUT color filters, and overlays before encoding.
  • Sending video and audio streams to the RTMP/RTMPS server configured by the user.
  • Saving local preferences so the app can restore previous settings.

4. Camera Permission

The app requires camera permission to display camera preview and capture video input. This permission is used only when the camera feature is active.

5. Microphone Permission

The app may request microphone permission to capture audio for live streaming. If audio streaming is not enabled, the app does not use the microphone for streaming.

6. USB/UVC Device Access

When the user connects a USB/UVC camera or HDMI capture device, Android may ask for USB access permission. This permission is used only to receive video input from the USB device selected by the user.

7. Stream Key and Streaming Server

A stream key is sensitive information. RVTik StreamCam uses the stream key only to connect to the streaming server configured by the user, such as YouTube, TikTok Live, or another platform that supports RTMP/RTMPS streaming.

Users are responsible for keeping their stream key private and not sharing it publicly or with unauthorized parties.

8. Data Sent to Third-Party Platforms

When the user starts live streaming, video and audio data are sent directly to the RTMP/RTMPS destination configured by the user. The destination platform may have its own privacy policy and terms of service.

RVTik StreamCam does not control how third-party platforms process streaming data after the stream is received by those platforms.

9. Local Storage

The app may store configuration data locally on the user's device, such as resolution, bitrate, FPS, server URL, stream key, selected camera, LUT selection, and overlay settings. This local storage is used to improve usability and restore the user's previous configuration.

10. Data Sharing and Selling

RVTik StreamCam does not sell, rent, or trade user personal data. Streaming data is sent only to the destination configured directly by the user.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect app functionality and user configuration. However, users are responsible for keeping their stream keys, streaming accounts, server URLs, and devices secure.

12. Data Deletion

Locally stored app settings can be removed by clearing the app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app. For data that has already been sent to third-party streaming platforms, users should refer to the data deletion policies and tools provided by those platforms.

13. Children’s Privacy

RVTik StreamCam is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
